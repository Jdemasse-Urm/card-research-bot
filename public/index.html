Drop a card to get started

The bot reads the card from the photo (player, year, set, number, parallel, grade), pulls the most recent sold prices for every grade, searches the web for news and price moves, and tells you whether to grade it, hold it or sell it.

Covers baseball, basketball, football, Yu-Gi-Oh and One Piece.

Watchlist

Cards you're tracking. Prices re-check automatically once a day while the app is open (free — no AI).

Ask anything about the card market

Examples: “What are the chase cards in the newest Topps Chrome?” · “Is it a good time to sell Wembanyama rookies?” · “Which One Piece set has the best pull rates for manga rares?” · “What's PSA's current turnaround and cost?”

Settings

Paste your Anthropic API key. Get one at console.anthropic.com → API Keys (add a few dollars of credit under Billing). It's saved only on this computer in the app's .env file.
