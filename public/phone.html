Getting a good picture
  • Lay cards on something dark and plain, with a little space between them.
  • Hold the phone straight above, close enough that the cards fill the picture.
  • Even light, no glare: tilt away from a lamp or window if the gloss shines.
  • A binder page or 8–12 cards at once works. Shoot the back by flipping the whole page.
  • Slabs: switch to One side, or tap No back after the front.
